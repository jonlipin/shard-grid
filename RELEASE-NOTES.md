## 1.11.0 - 2026-11-18

### New
- **Only on warlocks.** Shard Grid now keeps out of the way on your other characters, so there is no need to switch it off for them: no grid, no alerts, no soulstone tracker and no minimap button. /shards still opens the options, and "Only on warlocks" under Display can be unticked on a character where you want the soulstone tracker anyway.

### Fixes
- **No gem sound on every cast.** With auto-delete on, loose shards are moved into the soul bag one per key press, and the game played its item sound for each move, whatever the sound options said. A shard spent by Shadowburn frees a slot, so every cast after it clicked. Shard Grid's own moves and deletes are now silent, and a move the game refuses is tried again after a few seconds, not on every key press.
