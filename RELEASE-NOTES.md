## 1.5.0 - 2026-10-09

### New
- Matches EllesmereUI. With EllesmereUI installed and its Blizzard Skins+ module on, Shard Grid takes on its look: the grid, summon and soulstone windows get its backdrop and border, buttons go flat, slots and icons go square, the soulstone bars get a flat fill on a dark track, and text uses the font you chose in EllesmereUI. The overflow colors, and the soulstone bars turning red in the last minute, are kept.
- It can be switched off in EllesmereUI's own options, under Blizzard Skins+ > Window Skins > Third-Party Addons (takes a reload).
- Without EllesmereUI nothing changes. It is optional, not a requirement.
- `/shards debug` has a "skin" line saying which look is in use, and why when it is not the one you expected.
